# Sources for v0.4.14

Official source family: United Nations Statistics Division (UNSD), Classifications on economic statistics.

- ISIC Rev. 5 is published with structure and explanatory notes:
  https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ
- ISIC Rev. 4 class detail template:
  https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Detail/EN/27/{code}
- ISIC Rev. 5 class detail template:
  https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/EN/2095/{code}
- ISIC Rev. 4 -> Rev. 5 official correspondence remains the v0.4.13 source:
  https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/tables/ISIC/ISIC_Rev4_to_ISIC_Rev5_Correspondence_Table-17Jan2025.xlsx

The official explanatory notes define the content and scope of categories. v0.4.14 therefore treats title/definition comparison as a deterministic semantic audit, separate from the official correspondence itself.

No claim of semantic equivalence is made solely from matching codes or matching graph topology.
